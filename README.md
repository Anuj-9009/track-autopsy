## ✨ Core System Features

### 🗂️ 1. Case Registry (Cold Case Dossier)
The primary inquest record for certified audio cadavers:
* **Interactive Forensic Query**: Input any song title and artist. The system runs an autonomous inquest pulling real musicological facts or matching instant preset cases (Gotye, Smash Mouth, The Chainsmokers, PSY).
* **Live Spotify Metadata Integration**: Queries the Spotify Search API via client credentials flow to fetch official high-resolution album artwork, track duration, and release cataloging.
* **Chart Biopsy Milestone**: Analyzes the track's real Billboard Hot 100 peak position, longevity records, or multi-platinum milestones (e.g., *"Peaked at No. 1 on Billboard Hot 100 for 8 consecutive weeks, topping charts in 26 countries"*).
* **Transcription of Fatal Earworm Hook (Audio Evidence Item 00-A)**: A dedicated typewriter index card isolating the lethal chorus or hook lyric responsible for acute neural repetition.
* **Official Disposal Order**: Formulates the legally binding internment zone for the track's master files (e.g., *Class-IV Commercial Deadpool*, *Muzak Low-Pass Transcode Facility*).
* **Dynamic Satirical Artist Credentials**: Automatically awards the artist an absurd, genre-specific doctorate (e.g., *Dr. Gotye, D.A.P. — Doctor of Acoustic Pathology*; *Dr. Smash Mouth, Ph.D. — Doctor of Cinema Soundtracks*; *Dr. PSY, D.V.C. — Doctor of Viral Choreography*).
* **Archival Action Suite**:
  * **Print Dossier**: Dedicated print styling that generates a sterile, high-contrast black-and-white physical forensic report.
  * **Share Verdict**: Formats a clinical coroner summary and copies it directly to the system clipboard.
  * **Exhume Another**: Instant reset to inspect the next deceased track.

---

### 🔬 2. Waveform Dissection (Laboratory Oscilloscope)
An interactive frequency autopsy room inspecting the acoustic corpse:
* **Real-Time HTML5 Canvas Oscilloscope**: Simulates live analog cathode-ray tube oscilloscope sweeps with dual synchronized audio waves, animated scanlines, and grid reticles.
* **Interactive Wave Controls**: Toggle real-time sweep animation (`PLAY / PAUSE`) and select variable inspection speeds (`0.5X SLOW`, `1.0X STD`, `2.0X FAST`).
* **Specimen Master Format Display**: Identifies the original physical tracking medium (e.g., *2-inch 24-track analog tape digitized to 24-bit/96kHz Pro Tools Session*).
* **Isolated Fatal Hook Stem [Terminal Neural Imprint]**: Highlights the exact vocal stem or riff that caused auditory burnout.
* **4-Band Discrete Frequency Diagnostics**:
  * **20–60 Hz (Sub-Bass)**: Real instrumentation analysis (e.g., 808 subs, synthetic kick drums, bass guitars).
  * **200–500 Hz (Low-Mids)**: Acoustic guitar strumming, keyboard mud, or sync-license chord beds.
  * **2–4 kHz (Presence)**: Lead vocal formant saturation, megaphone filters, and transient fatigue.
  * **10–20 kHz (High Air)**: Cymbals, open hi-hats, tambourines, and lossy digital encoding hiss.
* **Integrated Loudness (LUFS) Meter**: Displays the true dynamic compression ceiling (e.g., `-7.1 LUFS` Brickwall Squash).
* **Degradation Timeline**: Traces the song's lifecycle from initial studio recording through viral cultural explosion to eventual terminal autopsy.

---

### 📦 3. Chain of Custody (Evidence Locker & Transfer Ledger)
A physical police evidence room tracking the seizure and storage of master recordings:
* **Primary Case Docket**: Cites official seizure origin (e.g., *The Barn Studios, Victoria, Australia* or *Stankonia Studios, Atlanta, GA*), lead custodian, and security classification.
* **4-Tier Evidence Transfer Ledger**:
  * **Item 01-A**: Original 2-inch multi-track studio master session tape.
  * **Item 01-B**: Radio broadcast promo CD-R marked with DJ automation cue times.
  * **Item 01-C**: Digital lossy AAC / MP3 streaming distribution transcode.
  * **Item 01-D**: Warped 7-inch vinyl single (45 RPM) with catalog run-out matrix etching.
* **Evidence Bag Inspection Cards**: Recreates vintage manila evidence envelopes with grommets, eyelets, barcodes, and forensic condition notes.
* **Custodian Oath of Isolation (Section 9)**: Statutory certification sworn under oath by the artist under their customized academic degree.
* **Interactive Mechanical Rubber Stamp**: Affix a red ink `[RESTRICTED ACCESS ARCHIVE]` seal with tactile sound and status updates.

### 📜 4. Coroner Protocols & Statutory Regulations (Directive 84-M)
The official statutory manual and regulatory criteria for declaring musical flatline:
* **Active Inquest Specimen Banner**: Dynamically synchronizes with the active case, citing its case number, statutory ICD diagnostic code, and prescribed disposal route.
* **The 5 Statutory Criteria for Cultural Death**:
  1. *The Supermarket Frequency Threshold (§ 18-SUB-MKT)*: Involuntary grocery store and elevator playback without listener consent.
  2. *Acoustic Saturation Ceiling (§ 44-LUFS-MAX)*: Loss of dynamic range exceeding -7.0 LUFS.
  3. *Algorithmic Asphyxiation (§ 92-FEED-AUTO)*: Submersion beneath automated autoplay queues with under 1% organic search volume.
  4. *Meme Parody Critical Mass (§ 61-VIR-MUT)*: Exhaustion caused by over 2,500 short-form video parodies and ukulele covers.
  5. *Terminal Silence / Flatline Certification (§ 05-FLAT-ZERO)*: Zero unprompted mentions across 180 consecutive calendar days.
* **Live Searchable ICD-Audio Diagnostic Directory**:
  * Filters across certified codes (`SA-101`, `SA-109`, `SA-204`, `SA-312`, `SA-408`, `SA-515`, etc.).
  * **Active Specimen Highlighting**: Automatically injects and highlights the active song's specific pathology with a distinctive `[SPECIMEN]` badge.
* **Disposal Ordinances**: Directives for internment to *Zone-A (Muzak Substation)*, *Zone-B (TV Re-Run Purgatory)*, or *Zone-C (Bar Jukebox Quarantine)*.
* **Rules for Case Exhumation**: Legal statutory conditions under which a dead track may be revived (Indie Auteur Placement, Subcultural Adoption, 25-Year Decadal Reset).
* **Interactive Inquest Petition Modal (Form IP-12)**: Allows users to submit new suspected dead tracks for formal coroner audit.

## 🧠 Dual-Engine Intelligence & Zero-Failure Architecture

The application is engineered to guarantee 100% uptime with zero crashes, whether connected to live AI models or completely offline:

1. **Google Gemini 3.6 Flash Model (`@google/genai`)**:
   * Uses structured JSON schema outputs (`Type.OBJECT`) for strict schema compliance.
   * Prompts enforce real Billboard historical milestones, authentic studio locations, verifiable release timelines, and accurate instrumentation diagnostics.
   * Generates bespoke satirical degrees, terminal hook lyrics, and statutory ICD codes tailored uniquely to the searched song.

2. **Spotify Web API Client Credentials Integration**:
   * Connects via Spotify API to fetch real album art, track duration, and release metadata.
   * Auto-refreshes bearer tokens securely.

3. **Deterministic Procedural Fallback Engine (`utils.ts`)**:
   * If API keys are omitted or network requests fail, an internal hash-based procedural generator computes mathematically consistent forensic dossiers.
   * Guarantees that every view, oscilloscope band, evidence ledger, and protocol directory displays complete, rich data without throwing runtime exceptions.

4. **In-Browser Configuration Modal**:
   * Users can enter their own Google Gemini API key or Spotify Client ID/Secret directly in the UI.
   * Credentials are saved locally to `localStorage` and never transmitted to external third parties.

## 🚀 How to Run & Operate Track Autopsy

Follow these step-by-step instructions to get the application running locally in under 2 minutes.

---

### 📋 Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure your system has:
* **Node.js** (version `18.0.0` or higher) — check with:
  ```bash
  node -v
  ```
* **npm** (version `9.0.0` or higher) or **pnpm** / **yarn** — check with:
  ```bash
  npm -v
  ```
* A modern web browser (Google Chrome, Brave, Safari, Firefox, Edge).

---

### 📥 1. Clone & Install Dependencies

Open your terminal and clone the repository:

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/Anuj-9009/track-autopsy.git

# Navigate into the project folder
cd track-autopsy

# Install all required dependencies
npm install
```

---

### ⚙️ 2. Configure Credentials (3 Flexible Modes)

You can run Track Autopsy in whichever mode best fits your setup:

#### 🟢 Mode A: Zero-Config / Offline Mode (No Keys Required)
> **Fastest way to test!** If you do not provide any API keys, the application automatically activates its **Deterministic Procedural Generator**. It algorithmically computes 100% complete, authentic forensic dossiers, frequency analyses, and chain-of-custody ledgers offline without making external network calls.

#### 🟡 Mode B: In-App UI Configuration (Recommended for Quick Testing)
1. Start the app with `npm run dev` and open `http://localhost:5173` in your browser.
2. In the top navigation bar, click the **`Config`** button (gear icon ⚙️).
3. Paste your **Google Gemini API Key** and/or **Spotify Client ID & Secret** directly into the modal and click **Save**.
4. Keys are saved locally into your browser's `localStorage` and are **never** committed to version control.

#### 🔵 Mode C: Local `.env.local` File (For Developers)
1. Copy the example environment configuration template:
   ```bash
   cp .env.example .env.local
   ```
2. Open `.env.local` in your code editor and fill in your keys:
   ```env
   # Spotify Developer API Credentials (https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard)
   VITE_SPOTIFY_CLIENT_ID=your_spotify_client_id_here
   VITE_SPOTIFY_CLIENT_SECRET=your_spotify_client_secret_here

   # Google Gemini API Key (https://aistudio.google.com/)
   VITE_GEMINI_API_KEY=your_gemini_api_key_here
   ```
   *(Note: `.env.local` is strictly excluded by `.gitignore` so your keys stay private).*

---

### 🔑 How to Obtain Free API Keys (Under 2 Minutes)

* **Google Gemini API Key (100% Free)**:
  1. Visit **[Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/)**.
  2. Sign in with your Google account.
  3. Click **"Get API key"** $\rightarrow$ **"Create API key"**.
  4. Copy your key (starts with `AIzaSy...`).

* **Spotify Developer Credentials (100% Free)**:
  1. Visit the **[Spotify Developer Dashboard](https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard)** and log in.
  2. Click **"Create App"**.
  3. Enter App Name: `Track Autopsy`, Description: `Music Coroner`, Redirect URI: `http://localhost:5173`.
  4. Navigate to your app's **Settings** tab to copy your **Client ID** and **Client Secret**.

### 🩺 4. How to Use the Forensic Interface

Once the app is running in your browser:

1. **Conduct an Autopsy**:
   * Click one of the historical archive presets (**Gotye**, **Smash Mouth**, **The Chainsmokers**, **PSY**) for instant retrieval.
   * Or enter **any** track title and artist into the dossier blotter (e.g. Track: `Hey Ya!`, Artist: `Outkast`) and click **`EXAMINE SPECIMEN`**.
2. **Review the Cold Case Dossier (`Case Registry`)**:
   * Inspect the official **Chart Biopsy** showing real Billboard Hot 100 historical peak and weeks at #1.
   * Read the **Transcription of Fatal Earworm Hook** on typewriter index card `00-A`.
   * Check the **Official Disposal Order** assigning the track to its mandated internment zone.
   * Verify the legal attestation signed under the artist's satirical medical degree (e.g., *Dr. Gotye, D.A.P.*).
3. **Inspect the Frequency Autopsy (`Waveform Dissection`)**:
   * Click **`Waveform Dissection`** in the header.
   * Toggle the real-time CRT oscilloscope (`PLAY` / `PAUSE`) and switch sweep speeds (`0.5X`, `1.0X`, `2.0X`).
   * Review the 4-band acoustic spectrum (Sub-Bass, Low-Mids, Presence, High Air) mapping to actual song instrumentation.
4. **Audit Seized Evidence (`Chain of Custody`)**:
   * Click **`Chain of Custody`** in the header.
   * Examine the 4-tier transfer ledger and vintage evidence bag cards (Master Tape, Radio Promo CD-R, Warped Vinyl 45).
   * Click the red **`AFFIX REGULATORY STAMP`** button to mechanically certify the Section 9 Custodian Oath of Isolation.
5. **Consult Statutory Law (`Coroner Protocols`)**:
   * Click **`Coroner Protocols`** in the header.
   * View the **Active Inquest Specimen Banner** linking your active case to statutory Directive 84-M.
   * Search the **ICD-Audio Diagnostic Directory** table where your active case is pinned with a `[SPECIMEN]` badge.
   * Click **`Submit Inquest Petition`** to submit a Form IP-12 petition for suspected audio corpses.
6. **Print or Share**:
   * Click **`PRINT DOSSIER`** in the footer bar for a clean, physical coroner printout.
   * Click **`SHARE VERDICT`** to copy a structured coroner summary directly to your clipboard.

### 🛠️ Troubleshooting & FAQ

* **Port 5173 is already in use?**  
  Run Vite on a custom port:
  ```bash
  npm run dev -- --port 3000
  ```

* **Getting an API error from Gemini?**  
  Ensure your API key is valid in `.env.local` or via the in-app **Config** modal. If no key is set or the model quota is exceeded, Track Autopsy automatically fails over to the internal mathematical generator with zero downtime.

* **TypeScript or CSS build errors?**  
  Clear the Vite cache and reinstall dependencies:
  ```bash
  rm -rf node_modules dist
  npm install
  npm run build
  ```
